Noto Sans Arabic, with modified vertical metrics.

Source:
- https://github.com/notofonts/arabic/releases/tag/NotoSansArabic-v2.013
- Files: NotoSansArabic/unhinted/otf/NotoSansArabic-{Regular,SemiBold}.otf

Modification:
- The hhea and OS/2 typo ascender and descender were set to BitcoinCoreSans's
  (969 and -241 units per 1000), and the line gaps to 0, so Arabic text that
  falls back to this font shares a baseline with BitcoinCoreSans. The OS/2 win
  metrics are unchanged so tall marks are not clipped.
