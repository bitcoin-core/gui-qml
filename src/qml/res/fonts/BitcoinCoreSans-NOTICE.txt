BitcoinCoreSans is a modified derivative of the Inter font family.

Source:
- https://github.com/rsms/inter

Modification:
- The default numeral glyphs were frozen to tabular figures with slashed zero
  by applying the OpenType `tnum` and `zero` features to the bundled Regular
  and Semi Bold OTFs.
- Inter's default numerals are already lining figures, so there was no `lnum`
  feature to freeze in the source build.

Renaming:
- The family was renamed to `BitcoinCoreSans` to avoid redistributing a
  modified font under the Reserved Font Name `Inter`.
